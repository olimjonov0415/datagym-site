#Ketma-ket kunlar

Window darsida ketma-ket kunlar kun raqamidan ROW_NUMBER ni ayirib topilgan edi. Haqiqiy sanalarda ham usul shu, faqat kun raqami emas, sananing o'zidan ayiriladi. #Bugungi sana

Joriy sana va vaqtni olish:

-- PostgreSQL
SELECT CURRENT_DATE, CURRENT_TIMESTAMP;

-- SQLite
SELECT date('now'), datetime('now'), date('now', 'localtime');

SQLite'da 'now' Grinvich vaqti (UTC) bo'yicha. Toshkentda ertalab soat 5 gacha date('now') kechagi sanani beradi, mahalliy vaqt uchun 'localtime' qo'shiladi. PostgreSQL'da CURRENT_DATE ulanishda sozlangan vaqt mintaqasi bo'yicha ishlaydi.

Bunday so'rovning natijasi har kuni boshqacha. Shuning uchun hisobot va testlarda "bugun" ko'pincha aniq sana bilan beriladi: "bugun 2024-06-30 deb hisoblang" yoki jadvaldagi bitta qatorda. Shart shunday berilgan bo'lsa, so'rovga ham CURRENT_DATE emas, aynan shu sana yoziladi.

Nima kerak Qanday yoziladi
Oraliq (vaqt ustunida) created_at >= '2024-03-01' AND created_at < '2024-04-01'
Yil, oy, kun PostgreSQL: EXTRACT(MONTH FROM d)::int, SQLite: CAST(strftime('%m', d) AS INTEGER)
Ikkala bazada oy CAST(SUBSTR(CAST(d AS TEXT), 6, 2) AS INTEGER)
Chorak (oy + 2) / 3; PostgreSQL'da EXTRACT(QUARTER FROM d) ham
Oy kaliti '2024-03' SUBSTR(CAST(d AS TEXT), 1, 7); PostgreSQL: TO_CHAR(d, 'YYYY-MM'), SQLite: strftime('%Y-%m', d)
Hafta kuni (0 yakshanba) PostgreSQL: EXTRACT(DOW FROM d), SQLite: CAST(strftime('%w', d) AS INTEGER)
Dushanbadan tartib (dow + 6) % 7
Soat CAST(SUBSTR(CAST(ts AS TEXT), 12, 2) AS INTEGER); PostgreSQL: EXTRACT(HOUR FROM ts), SQLite: strftime('%H', ts)
Vaqtsiz kun date(ts)
Kun qo'shish PostgreSQL: d + 7, SQLite: date(d, '+7 days')
Oy qo'shish PostgreSQL: CAST(d + INTERVAL '1 month' AS DATE), SQLite: date(d, '+1 month') (oy oxirida natija boshqa)
Oyning oxirgi kuni PostgreSQL: CAST(DATE_TRUNC('month', d) + INTERVAL '1 month - 1 day' AS DATE), SQLite: date(d, 'start of month', '+1 month', '-1 day')
Kunlar farqi PostgreSQL: d2 - d1, SQLite: julianday(d2) - julianday(d1)
Soatlar farqi PostgreSQL: EXTRACT(EPOCH FROM t2 - t1) / 3600, SQLite: (unixepoch(t2) - unixepoch(t1)) / 3600.0
Kunlar qatori WITH RECURSIVE: PostgreSQL'da d + 1, SQLite'da date(d, '+1 day'); PostgreSQL'da generate_series ham
Ketma-ket kunlar PostgreSQL: day - CAST(ROW_NUMBER() OVER (...) AS INTEGER), SQLite: date(day, '-' || ROW_NUMBER() OVER (...) || ' days')
Bugun PostgreSQL: CURRENT_DATE, SQLite: date('now') (UTC bo'yicha)
